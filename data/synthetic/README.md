# Synthetic fixtures

T2 uses the supplied deterministic corpus generated by `generate_synthetic.py`: 20 PDFs across the invented alpha, beta, gamma and delta layouts, with labelled debit rows in `expected_candidates.csv` and provenance in `MANIFEST.csv`.

`SYNTHETIC_DATA_EXPLAINED.md` documents the generator line by line. The corpus exercises grid and no-grid layouts, signed amounts, brackets, wrapped narrations, repeated headers and Indian digit grouping.

Real or redacted statements do not belong here.
